QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my classroom or academic block?

Check your current timetable on the official UMS portal for the assigned block and room. You can then search that block in the CampusHub location directory or an external map. Ask your mentor or staff to confirm the correct entrance if unclear.

Where can I check my timetable?

Use LPU's official UMS portal and the directions provided by your department. Your timetable may change, so rely on the official source rather than a saved screenshot.

What if the map points to the wrong place?

External maps can be inaccurate, particularly for large campuses and building entrances. Confirm the destination with current university signage or an authorised staff member. CampusHub does not claim to provide verified turn-by-turn campus routing.

Who should I ask if I am confused?

Start with your assigned faculty mentor, department office, or other officially designated university support channel. For general contact details, use the LPU official contact page linked above.

Do I have to complete all 15 checklist tasks?

No. This is a suggested orientation guide created for a student design-thinking project, not an official LPU requirement. Use only the steps relevant to you.

Is CampusHub an official LPU website?

No. CampusHub is an independent student-made academic prototype for the “First Fifteen Days” design-thinking project. For authoritative university information, use LPU's official services.
